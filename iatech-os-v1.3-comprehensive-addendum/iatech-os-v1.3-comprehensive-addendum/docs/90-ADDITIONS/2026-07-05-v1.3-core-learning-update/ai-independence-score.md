# AI Independence Score

## Purpose

The AI Independence Score helps tutors understand whether a learner is becoming more capable or merely more dependent on AI.

## Levels

### Level 1: AI-Led

AI generates most of the result. Learner observes and asks questions.

Appropriate for early Experience stage.

### Level 2: AI-Guided

Learner works with AI support. Learner makes some decisions.

### Level 3: Learner-Led With AI Support

Learner leads the work. AI is used for explanation, review, debugging, and suggestions.

### Level 4: Independent Builder

Learner can build and explain independently. AI is used mainly for productivity, review, and improvement.

## Product Implication

Submissions and reflections should capture:

- What AI helped with
- What learner did independently
- What learner understands
- What learner can explain

## Important Principle

The goal is not to eliminate AI use. The goal is to increase independent capability.
