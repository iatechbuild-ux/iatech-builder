# Student Handbook

## Your promise

You are here to **think, build, test, improve, present, and reflect** — and to make real things that help real people.

## How you'll work

- Every mission starts with a **person and a problem**, not a language.
- You'll learn a concept right when you need it, then use it immediately.
- You'll build something that *runs* — and sometimes deploy it so anyone can open it.
- You'll explain what you built. If you can explain it, you understand it.

## The rules

1. **Try before asking for the answer.** Struggle is part of learning.
2. **Never copy AI blindly.** Use it, then find what it got *wrong*.
3. **Always explain your work** in your own words.
4. **Improve after feedback.** Revision is not failure — it's how building works.
5. **Help others** once you understand something.
6. **Protect your privacy** online.

## Using AI the IATECH way

AI is a **collaborator, not a boss.** For every AI-practice step you'll show:
- the prompt you used,
- what the AI gave you,
- **one thing it got wrong or missed**, and
- how you checked or improved it.

That last part is the real skill. Anyone can paste an answer; you're learning to *judge* it.

## AI safety (never break these)

Never share with any AI tool: passwords · your home address · phone numbers · private family details · private school data · personal documents. If you're unsure, ask your tutor first.

## Getting help

Stuck is normal. Tell your tutor *what you tried* and *where you got stuck* — that's how you get the best help, and it's a skill in itself.
