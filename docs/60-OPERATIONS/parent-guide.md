# Parent / Guardian Guide

## What IATECH Builder is

It is not only teaching your child to code. It is developing a **problem solver who thinks differently** — someone who can look at a real situation, figure out the actual problem, and build something that helps.

## What your child will do

- Learn by building real projects, not just watching lessons.
- Use AI tools *responsibly and critically* — learning to catch when the AI is wrong.
- Build websites and simple programs, and deploy at least one online.
- Explain and present what they built.
- Receive human tutor feedback and improve.
- Grow a portfolio of real work.

## What to look for (better than grades)

- Growing confidence and independence.
- Completed projects they can show you.
- The ability to *explain* what they built and why.
- Better problem-solving habits at home.
- Responsible, careful use of AI.

## Your dashboard shows

Projects completed · plain-language skill progress (e.g., "Critical Thinking: approaching Independence") · tutor comments · attendance · next milestone. No confusing technical metrics — just real progress.

## How to support (no tech skills needed)

- Ask: "What did you build? Whose problem does it solve?"
- Ask them to *explain* it to you — explaining is how learning sticks.
- Celebrate improvement, not just perfection.
- Encourage safe AI use: remind them never to share passwords, your address, phone numbers, or private family information with any AI tool.

## Your child's privacy

Your child's work is **private by default.** You see it because your link to your child was verified by an admin. Nothing is shared publicly without consent. See [`safeguarding-and-data-policy.md`](safeguarding-and-data-policy.md) for exactly what we collect, why, and how we protect it.
