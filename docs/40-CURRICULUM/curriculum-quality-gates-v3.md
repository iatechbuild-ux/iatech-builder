# Curriculum Quality Gates v3

## Course publication gate

A course cannot be advertised as outcome-ready until every item is true:

- [ ] Exit performance is observable and independently assessable.
- [ ] Entry diagnostic and prerequisite route exist.
- [ ] Six to ten essential competencies are sequenced.
- [ ] At least three authentic mission contexts exist.
- [ ] A guided example, deliberate practice, integrated mission, and transfer capstone exist.
- [ ] Every essential competency maps to named evidence and rubric checks.
- [ ] Independence requires a second context and reduced assistance.
- [ ] Critical safety/privacy/accessibility conditions are explicit.
- [ ] AI disclosure and verification are built into relevant submissions.
- [ ] Low-bandwidth and 360px alternatives are documented.
- [ ] Tutor guide, answer notes, common misconceptions, and calibration samples exist.
- [ ] Time estimate has been piloted with at least three representative learners.
- [ ] A tutor has delivered the course without undocumented intervention.
- [ ] Learner feedback has produced at least one recorded revision.

## Mission publication gate

- [ ] Starts with a real person/process problem and a driving question.
- [ ] Declares target competencies separately from prerequisites.
- [ ] Defines the artifact, audience, constraints, and success measures.
- [ ] Uses all required mission stages with observable learner actions.
- [ ] Contains just-in-time lessons and practice, not headings alone.
- [ ] Requires normal, edge, and relevant safety tests.
- [ ] Provides learner choice without changing the assessed standard.
- [ ] Includes critique, revision, reflection, and explanation.
- [ ] Specifies assistance band and AI boundaries by stage.
- [ ] Lists required evidence in reviewable units.
- [ ] Uses Nigerian English, local context, and pathway-appropriate reading level.
- [ ] Avoids unnecessary personal information and unsafe external sharing.

## Lesson publication gate

- [ ] One measurable objective.
- [ ] Activates or checks prerequisite knowledge.
- [ ] Shows a correct model and at least one non-example/common error.
- [ ] Includes prediction, retrieval, comparison, debugging, or construction—not reading alone.
- [ ] Gives immediate feedback or tutor checking guidance.
- [ ] Connects directly to the next mission action.
- [ ] Has a phone-readable alternative and accessible content structure.
- [ ] Ends with a short check that determines proceed/practise/reteach.

## Portfolio claim gate

A public claim must be narrower than or equal to the evidence. Use:

- “Explored” for Awareness.
- “Built with guidance” for Application.
- “Built independently” only after approved transfer evidence.
- “Can guide others” only after witnessed Leadership evidence.

Never infer competence from attendance, time, lesson completion, AI output, or an unreviewed upload.

## Pilot decision rubric

After each cohort, review:

| Signal | Investigate when |
|---|---|
| Completion | Fewer than 70% of appropriately placed learners complete the capstone |
| Revision | More than 50% fail the same requirement on first submission |
| Independence | Learners pass guided work but fewer than 60% pass transfer |
| Tutor consistency | Reviewer decisions differ by more than one mastery level |
| Time | Median effort is outside 75–150% of the estimate |
| Access | Device, bandwidth, disability, or language barriers prevent required evidence |
| Authenticity | Learners cannot identify a real audience or use for the artifact |

These thresholds trigger curriculum investigation, not automatic blame of learners or tutors.
