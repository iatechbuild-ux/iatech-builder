# Lesson Template

> Restored in v2.0 (deleted in v1). A lesson is short, ordered, and introduces exactly the concept the next build step needs — no more.

Copy this to author a lesson inside a mission.

## Title
Short and concrete.

## Mission
Which mission this lesson serves, and which build step it unlocks.

## Objective
"By the end, students can ___." One observable capability, mapped to a competency.

## Opening story
A brief real-world situation that motivates the concept (2–4 sentences). Problem-first, even at lesson level.

## Concept
Explain the one concept, simply, at the pathway's reading level. Resist teaching two concepts.

## Worked example
One clear example, fully shown. For code, include predict-the-output before the reveal.

## Mini practice (activity)
A short task: predict-output, debug-this, or compare-two-options. Learner does, not just reads.

## Build step
Exactly how this lesson moves the mission's artifact forward. The learner should apply the concept immediately.

## Tutor questions
Facilitation prompts (Socratic, not answer-giving):
- What do you notice?
- Why does this matter for our problem?
- What could go wrong?

## Common mistakes
The two or three specific errors learners make here, and the correction.

## Reflection
What did you learn? What will you improve? (Feeds the mission-level reflection.)

## Authoring checklist
- [ ] One concept only.
- [ ] Opening story is problem-first.
- [ ] Includes a *doing* activity, not just reading.
- [ ] Ends by advancing the artifact.
- [ ] Reading level matches the pathway.
