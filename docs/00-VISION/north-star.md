# North Star

## Statement

Build the simplest, lowest-cost, tutor-guided platform that helps learners think critically, solve real problems, build and deploy working software, and develop durable future-ready skills — without depending on paid AI APIs in the MVP.

## The one sentence that decides scope

> Does this help a learner **think, solve, build, deploy, reflect, or present** — or help a tutor facilitate, a parent see real progress, or an admin protect quality and safety?

If no, it is not in the MVP.

## Graduate identity

> Solves problems and thinks differently.

## What we are building

A guided build environment. A learner moves from curiosity to capability along a repeatable arc:

Notice a problem → understand its context and people → think clearly → learn just what's needed → build → test → improve → deploy → present → reflect.

## What we are explicitly *not* building

- Not a coding academy that teaches syntax for its own sake.
- Not a generic LMS or content library.
- Not an AI tutor or chatbot.
- Not a certificate mill.

## How we measure the North Star

We are on course when a typical learner, by the end of a pathway, can show:
- at least one deployed project with a live link,
- a portfolio of runnable artifacts tagged by competency,
- a spoken or written explanation of what they built and why,
- evidence of improvement between a first and a revised version,
- and responsible, *critical* use of AI (they can say what the AI got wrong).

Vanity metrics we will not optimize for: lessons completed, time-in-app, or certificates issued.

## The timeless principle

**Technology serves people.** This stays true when languages, frameworks, AI systems, and job markets change. Everything else in these docs is downstream of it.
