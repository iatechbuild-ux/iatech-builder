# Vision

## Vision statement

To become a leading African builder platform that develops future tech enthusiasts who identify real problems, design practical solutions, build useful technology, and improve business and community processes.

## The 10-year view

In a decade, IATECH learners should be recognizable by how they work, not by a credential:

- they start from people and problems, not tools;
- they can build and ship a working solution;
- they use AI as a collaborator and can name its failure modes;
- they improve business and community processes wherever they land;
- they teach themselves new technologies without waiting for a course.

## Strategic position

IATECH does not win by cloning Code.org, Scratch, Khan Academy, or a bootcamp. Those teach *content* or *syntax*. IATECH teaches *solution-minded building*, tutor-guided, grounded in African and global realities.

Our moat is not a feature — features are copyable. The moat is the **coherence of the model**: problem-first missions, human facilitation, verified AI literacy, portfolio evidence, and low-cost reach on low-end devices, all reinforcing one another.

## Who we serve first

Learners roughly 10–18 in African contexts — many on shared or low-end devices with intermittent connectivity, many without prior structured computer access. We design for that learner first; everyone with better conditions is served automatically.

## What would make this vision fail

Naming these so we can steer around them:

1. **Drifting into an LMS.** Adding content and dashboards until the build-first soul is gone.
2. **Tutor quality variance.** The model leans on good facilitation; inconsistent tutors quietly degrade outcomes.
3. **AI-practice becoming theater.** Learners copy-paste; the critical-thinking goal evaporates.
4. **Cost creep.** Reintroducing paid dependencies before the model is proven.
5. **Content bottleneck.** The software ships but there aren't enough excellent missions to run it.

Each failure mode has an owner and a mitigation elsewhere in these docs.
