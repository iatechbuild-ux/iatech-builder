# Mission

## Mission statement

IATECH Consult exists to develop problem solvers who use technology to improve businesses, communities, and lives.

## Operational mission

Help learners master future-ready skills through real-life missions, guided practice, project-based building, responsible AI use, and portfolio development — delivered by human tutors and supported by simple, low-cost software.

## The problem we are correcting

Most technology education is taught backwards: theory, syntax, and stacks first; problems and people last (or never). Learners emerge able to recite but unable to solve. IATECH reverses the order — **problem first, then only the tools the problem demands.**

## What this means in practice

- A mission never opens with "learn a loop." It opens with "a shopkeeper recounts stock every night and keeps making errors."
- A concept is introduced at the moment the build needs it, then immediately applied.
- Success is a working, explainable artifact — not a completed lesson.

## Why now

AI has made *information* nearly free. The scarce, valuable skills are now judgment, problem framing, verification, and the ability to turn an idea into a shipped thing. IATECH is built for exactly that shift.
