# Personas

Concrete personas beat abstract roles. Each has a name, a context grounded in the reference device/connectivity reality, a goal, frustrations, and the single job the product must nail for them.

## Amara — Student, 13, Explorer pathway

- **Context:** Shares an Android phone with a sibling; data bundle runs out mid-week; first structured exposure to building anything.
- **Goal:** Make something real she can show her family.
- **Frustrations:** Gets lost when a screen has too many options; loses work when the connection drops.
- **The one job:** *Always know the single next step*, and never lose a draft to a dropped connection.

## Tunde — Student, 16, Builder pathway

- **Context:** Comfortable on a laptop at a center twice a week; has built a couple of pages before.
- **Goal:** Build a portfolio that proves he can ship, for future opportunities.
- **Frustrations:** Bored by hand-holding; wants to deploy and be judged on real work.
- **The one job:** Give him missions with real stakes and a live URL at the end.

## Mrs. Bello — Tutor

- **Context:** Facilitates two cohorts; strong mentor, not a career programmer; time-poor.
- **Goal:** Run great sessions without inventing lessons or hunting for notes.
- **Frustrations:** Documents scattered outside the app; unclear which students need help; review overload.
- **The one job:** Open the app and see *today's lesson, who's stuck, and what's waiting for review* — all in one place.

## Mr. Okafor — Parent / Guardian

- **Context:** Wants to know the money and time are producing something; not technical.
- **Goal:** See real transformation, not jargon.
- **Frustrations:** Dashboards full of metrics that mean nothing to him.
- **The one job:** Show completed projects, plain-language skill progress, and tutor comments — nothing else.

## Zainab — Admin (IATECH staff)

- **Context:** Manages content quality, cohorts, tutor assignment, and safety.
- **Goal:** Keep learning quality high and children safe, at low operational effort.
- **Frustrations:** Chasing data; unclear safeguarding process; content bottlenecks.
- **The one job:** Publish and manage missions/cohorts, and act on safety flags, without engineering help.

## Design consequence

Every screen is judged against its persona's "one job." If a screen doesn't serve its persona's one job, it is redesigned or removed.
